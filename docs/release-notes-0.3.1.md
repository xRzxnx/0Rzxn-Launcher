# 0Rzxn_ Launcher 0.3.1

## English

- **Crystal menu.** Give Minecraft's title screen the launcher's style: three layouts, the instance background as a rotating panorama, and an instance card. It needs a mod loader. Turn it on in Settings › your instance › Game title screen. The launcher downloads FancyMenu and what it needs from Modrinth, and one click brings the classic menu back.
- **Missing mod dependencies.** The launcher now installs them for you.
- **A cleaner install.** After a manual install, the launcher now also finds older copies of itself that Windows does not list, removes them and repairs their shortcuts. Your instances, worlds and settings stay untouched.
